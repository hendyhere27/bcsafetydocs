7. Privacy Policy

BC Safety Docs collects only the information necessary to process your order, deliver your purchase or free download, and respond to you. This includes:

  • Email address — used to deliver your purchased document and respond to support requests
  • Free download signups — when you request a free template we collect your email address, which template you asked for, and the date. We use it to email your download link. If you tick the optional box on the form, we also add your email to our mailing list to send occasional WorkSafeBC compliance updates; if you leave it unticked we do not add you to that list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any update email or by writing to us
  • FireSmart self-check — if you request the emailed report from our FireSmart assessment tool, we collect your email address, the answers you select (roof, siding, outbuildings, water supply, surrounding fuels, land size), your score, and only the first three characters of your postal code. We never store the full postal code. We use these to send your report and to understand what the tool is used for. If you tick the optional box we also add your email to our mailing list to send occasional wildfire-safety and WorkSafeBC updates; if you leave it unticked we do not add you to that list. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data at any time
  • COR incentive calculator — if you request the emailed breakdown from our COR incentive calculator, we collect your email address, the figures you enter (annual payroll, optional number of workers, base rate) and the resulting estimate, plus a truncated, one-way hash of your network address used only to limit abuse. We email you the breakdown. If you tick the optional box we also add your email to our mailing list to send a few short COR-readiness emails; if you leave it unticked we do not add you to that list. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data at any time
  • COR gap check — if you request the emailed report from our COR gap check, we collect your email address, your answers to the 13 questions, your selected industry, and a truncated, one-way hash of your network address used only to limit abuse. We email you the report. If you tick the optional box we also add your email to our mailing list to send a few short COR-readiness emails; if you leave it unticked we do not add you to that list. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data at any time
  • Payment information — collected and processed by Stripe. BC Safety Docs does not receive or store card numbers or banking details
  • Purchase details — product purchased, amount, and date, retained for accounting and tax purposes

You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings or opt out through Google's ad settings. We keep free-download signup records until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. BC Safety Docs complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of British Columbia.
