Is this for you?

Built for small BC employers.

COR is a voluntary WorkSafeBC program. Employers certified through a sector certifying partner can earn an annual premium incentive — WorkSafeBC says up to 10% off base premiums (see how it's calculated, or estimate yours with our free calculator) — and in construction a COR is often what general contractors look for when prequalifying bidders. To get there you need a written safety program that matches how you actually work.

A good fit

  • BC employers with fewer than 20 workers — the size BCCSA's Small COR is designed for (19 or fewer)
  • Construction and general-industry contractors, where the permits and field templates in this kit apply directly
  • Owners writing their first safety program who want it cited to the right sections

Check first — or look elsewhere

  • Agriculture or tourism: the core documents apply, but your certifying partner has its own audit tool — compare before you buy
  • Forestry (SAFE Companies) and oil & gas (Energy Safety Canada): their audit structures differ and this kit isn't designed around them
  • 20 or more workers: a larger employer needs the full audit, an external auditor, and more depth than templates provide

Questions

Before you buy.

BCCSA helps small employers for free. Why buy this?

Use both. BCCSA is the certifying partner for construction. Its Regional Safety Advisors offer on-site support to members, and it publishes its own COR forms and resources. That help is worth using, and this kit is not a substitute for it. We are independent and not affiliated with BCCSA.

What the kit adds is the working paperwork: registers, logs and checklists cited to the OHS Regulation and the Workers Compensation Act, as editable Word files, so you can start keeping the records an audit looks for. BCCSA says there should be at least 6 months of supporting evidence, with 12 months recommended, and that evidence only exists if the records have actually been kept.

If BCCSA’s own forms already cover what you need, you may not need to buy anything. If you would rather start from one complete, cited, editable set than assemble your own, this saves you the drafting time. The same goes for your sector’s partner if you are not in construction, such as AgSafe for agriculture or go2HR for tourism.

Does this get me certified?

No. It gives you the written program documents. Certification is awarded through your sector's certifying partner (for example BCCSA for construction) after an audit of your actual program. We can't promise you'll pass, or that you'll receive any WorkSafeBC incentive.

Do I still need an audit?

Yes. You arrange your audit through your certifying partner. Small employers answer a reduced question set, but still need documentation touching every element, and a minimum score in each one.

Can I buy individual documents?

The ten COR program documents are sold only as this kit. The eight field templates in it are free to download on their own: Confined Space Entry Permit, JSA / FLHA, Hot Work Permit, Lockout / Tagout, Daily FLHA, Safe Work Procedure, Incident Report and Emergency Response Plan. The OHS Policy Statement is also free. What you pay for is the other COR documents and the element-by-element mapping.

How is it delivered?

Instantly, by email: a download link to a zip file of 18 Word (.docx) documents plus a short start-here guide and the bonus COR Audit Quick Reference.

What if my sector or size isn't listed as a good fit?

Look at your certifying partner's audit tool before buying and compare it with the coverage table above. Email info@bcsafetydocs.com if you're not sure — we'd rather tell you it isn't a fit.
