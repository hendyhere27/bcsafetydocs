Is this for you?

Built for small BC employers.

COR is a voluntary WorkSafeBC program. The kit gives you the written program. It does not certify you: you still pass an audit through your sector's certifying partner.

A good fit

  • BC employers with fewer than 20 workers
  • Construction and general-industry contractors, where the permits and field templates apply directly
  • Owners writing a first safety program who want it cited to the right sections

Check first, or look elsewhere

  • Agriculture or tourism: the core documents apply, but your certifying partner has its own audit tool
  • Forestry and oil & gas: their audit structures differ and the kit is not designed around them
  • 20 or more workers: you need the full audit and more depth than templates provide
