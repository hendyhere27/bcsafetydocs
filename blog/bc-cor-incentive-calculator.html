What could a COR be worth? A free calculator for BC employers.

October 9, 2026  ·  BC Safety Docs  ·  3 min read

WorkSafeBC pays an annual incentive to employers who hold a valid Certificate of Recognition (COR). The question most small employers ask first is simple: how much? We built a free calculator that answers it from WorkSafeBC's own published rules.

Try the COR incentive calculator →

How it works

Enter your annual payroll, optionally your number of workers, and your classification unit's base rate per $100 of payroll. The calculator shows an estimated incentive per year and over three years, applies the minimum and the per-worker earnings cap, and tells you where each figure comes from. If you want the step-by-step arithmetic, it will email you the full breakdown.

The rules behind the number

  • Formula. Assessable payroll × (classification unit base rate ÷ 100) × 10%, worked out for each classification unit in your COR.
  • Minimum. At least the lesser of $1,000 or 75% of the premiums you paid. For a small employer this is often the number that applies.
  • Per-worker cap. Assessable earnings are capped per worker: $127,500 for 2026.
  • Timing. You have to be certified first. WorkSafeBC starts considering payments in the second quarter of the year after certification and expects to credit your account by the end of June.
  • Conditions. A valid certificate, an active account, and no exceptions such as late payroll reporting, an administrative penalty or a health and safety conviction.

What it doesn't tell you

It is an estimate, not a promise. You still have to pass a certification audit through your sector's certifying partner and keep the certificate valid with annual maintenance audits. The figure also leaves out what getting certified costs: your time, any partner registration or program fees, an external auditor if you use one, and training for an internal auditor. And it uses your base rate, which is what WorkSafeBC's formula uses, not your net rate after experience rating.

Not sure where you stand?

The incentive only matters if you can get through an audit. Our free COR gap check takes two minutes: answer Yes, Partly or No for each of the 13 COR elements and see what is in place, what is missing, and which documents cover each gap. Several of those documents are free templates.
